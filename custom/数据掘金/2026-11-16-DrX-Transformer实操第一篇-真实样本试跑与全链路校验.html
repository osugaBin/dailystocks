DrX-Transformer 实操 · 第一篇：真实样本试跑与全链路校验DrX-Transformer in Practice, Part 1: First End-to-End Trial Run on Real Data and Full-Pipeline Validation

英文摘要Abstract

Part 1 of the series reports the first end-to-end trial run of the two-stage Transformer stock-selection system on real CSI 300 data from January 2024 — 22 trading days, 297 tradable constituents and 6,528 panel rows — with the validation results placed first. The data pipeline passed its Phase 1 acceptance checks with zero missing values across all core fields, under an explicit survivorship-bias warning. On a MacBook Air M4 with 32 GB of unified memory, the Metal backend accelerated forward inference by about 3.11 times and full training steps by about 3.50 times relative to CPU. In the purged out-of-sample window, the two-stage Transformer returned +1.02% net of all trading costs against -0.51% for the CSI 300, ahead of equal-weight, momentum, XGBoost and random forest baselines. All 13 automated tests passed, including a future-price perturbation test confirming zero look-ahead leakage. The sample is deliberately short: the run validates engineering correctness rather than establishing alpha.
