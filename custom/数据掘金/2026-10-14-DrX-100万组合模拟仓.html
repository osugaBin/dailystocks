DrX-100万组合模拟仓

英文摘要Abstract

This page documents a CNY 1,000,000 simulated portfolio that runs three independent strategy sleeves side by side. A CNY 300,000 sleeve trades Xinjinlu (000510.SZ) under the mechanical box-regression rule set; a CNY 400,000 value sleeve tracks six industry leaders for pullback entries; and a CNY 300,000 trend sleeve scans seven semiconductor and AI-theme names for right-side breakouts. Each sleeve keeps its own budget, signals and risk limits, and the combined book is marked to market every trading day with an interactive candlestick-style equity curve. In the current phase the value and trend sleeves record signals and valuations without auto-executing, so the page functions as an auditable lab notebook: every rule is stated in advance, every signal is logged, and performance can be attributed sleeve by sleeve. The design goal is discipline under drawdowns - position size and exits are decided by pre-committed formulas, not by intraday sentiment.
