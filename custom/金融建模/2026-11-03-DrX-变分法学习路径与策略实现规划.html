英文摘要Abstract

This is the roadmap for a self-study programme that connects three books: Zhang Gongqing's Lectures on the Calculus of Variations as the skeleton (it poses the problems), Oksendal's Stochastic Differential Equations as the toolbox (it supplies the rigorous language), and Nualart's Malliavin Calculus as the lift to infinite dimensions (differentiation on Wiener space, whose integration-by-parts formula computes Greeks without differentiating the payoff). The path runs in four stages of roughly three weeks each - classical variational methods and duality; stochastic analysis foundations; stochastic control and dual pricing; stochastic variational methods and extreme risk - each with an explicit pass criterion. Attached is an engineering plan of five milestones that turns the theory into running, simulated systems on this site: an HJB variational-inequality solver, a Malliavin-weight Greeks and hedging engine, a pairs-trading HJB simulation, a large-deviation risk gauge for the tracked stock universe, and a constrained portfolio solver - each shipped small, benchmarked, and subordinated to the existing trading discipline.
